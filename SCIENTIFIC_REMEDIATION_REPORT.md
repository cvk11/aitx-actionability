## Remaining limitations (honest)
1. **The verifier is still an LLM.** Quote grounding proves the passage exists, not that the model interpreted it correctly; a verifier can still accept a real but irrelevant passage.
2. **Paywalled/bot-protected full texts, patient tables and supplements are not accessible** (e.g. PMID 38844245's table). Such evidence is reported as "could not be assessed", which lowers completeness.
3. **Variant-notation matching** covers common HGVS/short forms and rsIDs; unusual spellings, legacy numbering or other transcripts' numbering can be missed (→ conservative applicability downgrade).
4. **Trial genotype gate** uses pattern-based gene extraction from registry text; unusual phrasing can be missed (no exclusion then; the verifier still checks eligibility text).
5. **Salvaged statements** are concatenations of verified claims — accurate but less readable; their frequency must be measured in the first run.
6. **No measured superiority over a plain Claude agent** (arm A vs B not yet run).
7. **Coverage queries are first-page (20 hits per family)**; truncation is recorded but further pages are reviewed only if the model asks. The dossier passed to the researchers is capped at 30k characters; the post-research coverage check is the safety net for hits that were cut.
8. **Follow-up is bounded** (one round, ≤14 requests); excess requests are logged as `followup_requests_truncated`, not researched.
9. **Token-based pre-call reservation is an estimate**, not a provider-side financial cap; a provider spend limit must be set in the Anthropic console.
